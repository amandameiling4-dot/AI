# Copyright and Legal Guidelines

## 1. Purpose
These guidelines help ensure that the application is developed and operated in a manner that respects copyright, licensing, and user rights.

## 2. Copyright Principles
- Respect third-party copyrights in code, assets, datasets, and generated content.
- Only use content that the team has rights to use or that is licensed appropriately.
- Maintain attribution when required by licenses.

## 3. User Generated Content
- Users retain rights to their original content unless they explicitly transfer them.
- The platform should clearly state how user content can be stored, processed, and used.
- Provide an opt-out or deletion mechanism for user content.

## 4. AI-Generated Content
- Disclose when content is AI-generated or AI-assisted.
- Avoid presenting AI output as human-authored without disclosure.
- Implement filters to reduce infringement, plagiarism, and unsafe output.

## 5. Compliance Requirements
- Follow regional laws for privacy, data protection, and consumer rights.
- Provide terms of service and a privacy policy.
- Record consent for data processing where applicable.

## 6. Recommended Controls
- Content moderation workflow
- License scanning for third-party assets
- Human review for high-risk content
- Audit logs for legal and compliance review
